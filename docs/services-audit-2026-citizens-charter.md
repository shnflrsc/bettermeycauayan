# Services Audit Against the 2026 Meycauayan Citizen's Charter

Audit date: 2026-08-11  
Source: `CITIZENS_CHARTER_2026_1ST_ED_MEYC.pdf` (546 PDF pages)  
Records audited: 26

## Executive summary

- 26 of 26 records were checked against their corresponding 2026 office sections.
- 25 records have an identifiable current counterpart or a documented successor service.
- 1 record, Emergency Shelter Assistance, was not found as a standalone service in the 2026 Charter.
- 2 existing business-permit records now belong to one coordinated BOSS workflow plus a separate online-renewal workflow.
- Medical Assistance and Burial Assistance are no longer accurately represented by the older 25-minute Social Case Study workflow alone. The 2026 Charter documents Mayor's Financial Assistance and CSWDO Social Case Study Report Issuance as related but distinct transactions.
- The existing records were accurately transcribed from older screenshots, but the 2026 Charter changes requirements, processing times, fees, responsible offices, and service titles extensively.

Status meanings:

- **Replace**: the 2026 workflow materially differs from the current record.
- **Expand**: the current core is recognizable but omits important 2026 requirements or stages.
- **Reconcile**: the old record maps to multiple current services or a differently organized workflow.
- **Verify/retire**: no standalone 2026 counterpart was found.

## Cross-cutting data issues

1. **Service numbers**  
   The Charter uses office-local numbered services. These can replace all `TBD` and placeholder values, but the number should be stored with an office context to avoid ambiguity (for example, `CCRO-8`, not merely `8`).

2. **Classification type**  
   The current TypeScript schema accepts only `Simple` or `Complex`, while the 2026 Charter also uses `Highly Technical`. The schema and filters must be expanded before importing the official classifications.

3. **Official page references**  
   Each record should store the Charter edition, PDF page range, printed Charter page range, and official office-local service number. A single website URL is not sufficiently precise for future audits.

4. **Source-version conflict**  
   Existing screenshots appear to come from an earlier Charter edition. The 2026 PDF should become the controlling source, while the screenshots can remain provenance for historical comparison.

5. **Appointments and delivery channel**  
   Do not default every transaction to `Walk-in`. The 2026 renewal workflow is online, inspections are scheduled, and several services have multi-day or posting stages. Appointment/delivery fields must be assigned from each table.

6. **Processing time model**  
   Store both active transaction time and elapsed turnaround time. Several 2026 services combine minutes/hours with scheduled inspections, posting periods, or multi-day approval.

7. **Fees**  
   Fee displays should preserve `None`, fixed fees, statutory formulas, and assessment-table references without coercing all null amounts to zero.

## Recommended correction sequence

1. Update the shared schema for `Highly Technical`, precise source citations, delivery channel, and active versus elapsed time.
2. Replace the two business-permit records and reconcile Medical/Burial Assistance first.
3. Correct Sanitary Permit, PWD ID, Solo Parent ID, and Anti-Rabies Vaccination because their current public guidance is materially incomplete or incorrect.
4. Replace the engineering, zoning, taxation, and civil-registry records office by office.
5. Confirm the status of Emergency Shelter Assistance with CSWDO before retaining it as current.
6. Regenerate the merged datasets and run a complete placeholder and schema validation pass.

## Audit scope note

This audit reports discrepancies only. It does not modify the 26 service source records or generated service datasets.
